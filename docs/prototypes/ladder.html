Ladder Lab

How should rank be shown to players? You play one or two seasons as one player in a population of 400: win, lose, let your real skill decide, take breaks, and roll into the next season. Underneath runs the rating we picked in ADR 0010 (Weng-Lin Thurstone-Mosteller). Pick a ladder structure and watch what the player is told, next to what the game actually knows.

Assumptions: solo 5v5 matches (ADR 0006), so the party bonus stays at 0 here. The matchmaker finds nine players near your hidden rating, a little loosely, as at about 200 CCU. Tiers are cut so that, by real skill, 15% of players are Bronze, 25% Silver, 27% Gold, 18% Platinum, 10% Diamond, 4% Master and 1% Champion. Background players play 0–6 matches a day and sometimes take breaks. Rewards follow ADR 0009: dated seasonal cosmetics only.

What the rules mean
Hidden rating
ADR 0010's rating: a mean and an uncertainty. It runs matchmaking. It moves on win or loss only, and its uncertainty grows a little each idle day and each new season.
Divisions and points
Each tier below Master has divisions III, II and I, 100 points each. Points carry over on promotion and demotion. A win pays more than a loss costs while your rank is below your hidden rating, and the reverse above it (Valorant and League of Legends call this convergence).
Progress bar
The same points, shown as a percentage through the division, with the reasons for each change listed under it (Overwatch 2 since Season 9).
Stars
Five stars per division. A win is +1 star, a loss −1. Below Diamond, a loss at the start of a tier costs nothing (floor protection), and three wins in a row pay +2. Far below your hidden rating, a win pays +1 more (rank boost). Stars do not look at the hidden rating otherwise (Brawl Stars).
Shown rating
The hidden rating itself, as a number (1500 = average, 50 per skill point), with its tier. While uncertain it says so (StarCraft II since patch 3.4, Splatoon X Power).
Placements
The first matches of an account show no rank, only a prediction. Afterwards the rank is set from the hidden rating, optionally one uncertainty low, and capped.
Decay
From Diamond up, each match banks 7 days (28 at most). Each day uses one. A day with an empty bank costs 50 points, down to Diamond III at worst (League of Legends).
Champion
The apex tier, a leaderboard. Either everyone in Master whose hidden rating clears the Champion line, or only the top 1% of active players. Both need 10 matches in the last 21 days.
Ranked-win rewards
A dated season cosmetic per tier. "Wins at rank": 10 wins at that tier or above. "Peak": every tier you reached. "Season end": every tier up to where you finished.
