# Carrying a crate is a commitment

A **carrier** moves at 0.6× speed, cannot fire, and drops the crate when hit. We chose that
in play ([#9](https://github.com/ALLiDoizCode/big_back/blob/prototype/core-feel/docs/prototypes/core-feel.md#verdict)) over a sidearm or melee while carrying, keeping the crate
through hits, and a light 0.85× slowdown. This makes hauling a team problem: carriers need
escorts, and any hit is a chance to steal. It's surprising for a shooter, and the squad
model ([#10](https://github.com/ALLiDoizCode/big_back/issues/10)) and production-loss rules
([#15](https://github.com/ALLiDoizCode/big_back/issues/15)) build on it, so don't "fix" it
by arming carriers without revisiting those.
